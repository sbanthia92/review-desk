# Leicester proved money no longer decides the Premier League

Every August the pundits tell us the richest clubs will win the league, and every August we nod along. We should stop. Leicester City won the 2015-16 Premier League title, and nobody who watched that team can still believe the wage bill writes the table.

Claudio Ranieri's side begun the season as 500-1 outsiders with the bookmakers. They finished ten points clear of second-placed Arsenal, and Jamie Vardy scored in eleven consecutive league matches along the way. The players was fitter, hungrier and better organised than anyone expected.

Ranieri never tried to out-spend anyone. He set his team up to absorb pressure and break at speed, and he picked his battles. If you know the enemy and know yourself, you need not fear the result of a hundred battles.

Since then, smaller clubs have copied the Leicester model and closed the gap on the big six. Mid-table clubs now win 40% more points against the top six than they did a decade ago.

If one club with a modest budget can win the league, then money clearly does not decide who wins. Its time the rest of the league stopped making excuses and started believing.
