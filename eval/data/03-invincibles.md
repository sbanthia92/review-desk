# The Invincibles are the greatest Premier League team ever

Every few years somebody tries to crown a new "greatest team", and every few years they are wrong. The answer has not changed since Arsène Wenger's Arsenal.

Wenger had arrived from Japanese side Nagoya Grampus in 1990 with a reputation for diet sheets and stretching routines. Within a decade he had built something English football had never seen. Arsenal went unbeaten for all 38 league games in 2004-05, a feat that have never been repeated in the Premier League. They won 26 games and drew 12, and Thierry Henry scored 30 league goals.

Most former players rank them above the Manchester United treble side of 1999. Their was no weakness in that side: Lehmann, Campbell, Vieira, Pirès, Bergkamp and Henry would walk into any team of any era.

Manchester City's 100-point season does not count, because City spent more money than Arsenal ever did. Points bought with oil money are not the same as points earned.

Unbeaten is unbeaten. Nobody else has done it, and until somebody does, the debate is closed.
