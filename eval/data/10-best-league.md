# The Premier League is the best league in the world, and it isn't close

Every weekend the stadiums is full, the football is relentless and anyone can beat anyone. The Premier League was founded in 1992, and in three decades it has become the league every player wants to test himself in.

The goals prove it. Alan Shearer is the Premier League's all-time top scorer with 206 goals, a record that shows how hard it is to score in this division. Manchester City became the first team to reach 100 points in 2018-19, and even that side dropped points at grounds nobody outside England has heard of.

Premier League players cover 15% more distance per match than players in any other league. Our life is frittered away by detail. You do not need a spreadsheet to see the intensity; you just need to watch.

The Premier League is the best league because it earns the most television money. Broadcasters pay for what people want to watch, and people want to watch this.

Anyone who says otherwise is supporting a league who's clubs cannot keep their best players.
