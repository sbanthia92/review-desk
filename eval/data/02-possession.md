# Possession is the only stat that matters

I have watched football for thirty years and I am done pretending otherwise: the team with the ball is the better team. Everything else is noise dressed up as analysis.

Look at the evidence. Spain won Euro 2008, the 2010 World Cup and Euro 2016 by passing opponents into submission. Pep Guardiola's Barcelona won every trophy available to them in 2009, and they did it with the ball at their feet for most of every match. Teams that average over 60% possession win nine out of ten matches.

Guardiola's teams dont just keep the ball, they suffocates you with it. Hence to fight and conquer in all your battles is not supreme excellence; supreme excellence consists in breaking the enemy's resistance without fighting.

Possession stats prove who the better team was, because if you have the ball, the other side cannot score. A club that wants to win should buy passers first and worry about everything else later.

So next time a pundit tells you a side "deserved" a win with 30% of the ball, change the channel.
