# VAR has ruined the matchday

I used to love the half-second after a goal. Now I spend it looking at the referee, waiting to find out whether I am allowed to be happy.

Football has been played at World Cups since the first tournament in Brazil in 1930, and for most of that time nobody asked a man in a van to re-referee the game. VAR was used at the 2018 World Cup in Russia, and the Premier League introduced it in the 2016-17 season. Since then the atmosphere in the ground have been sucked out.

Match-going fans now spend an average of 14 minutes per game waiting for VAR checks. Supporters are seeing less goals celebrated freely, because every celebration comes with an asterisk. The mass of men lead lives of quiet desperation.

Referees got along fine for 150 years without video, so the game never needed it. Mistakes were part of the drama, and we argued about them in the pub instead of on a screen.

Scrap it, and give the game back to the people in the stands.
